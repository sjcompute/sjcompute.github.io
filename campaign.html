Why We're Doing This

In Liberia, access to technology and digital education resources remains limited. Many students and communities lack the basic tools needed to develop crucial computer skills in our increasingly digital world.

We believe that every quality laptop no longer being used represents an opportunity. By refurbishing these devices with Linux and sending them to Liberia, we're not just recycling electronics; we're opening doors to education, connection, and future opportunities.

What Laptops Can You Donate?

We're looking for quality laptops that can serve students well in Liberia. The sweet spot? Windows 10 laptops that are still in good working condition but can't upgrade to Windows 11.

✅ Ideal Donations

  • Windows 10 laptops in good working condition
  • Laptops that can't upgrade to Windows 11
  • All major brands accepted (Dell, HP, Lenovo, etc.)
  • Laptops with at least 4GB RAM and functional hardware

🛡️ Our Process

We perform a secure, certified data wipe on every device and install a free, open-source Linux operating system, transforming it into a perfect tool for learning in Liberian schools.

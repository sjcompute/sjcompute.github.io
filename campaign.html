Why We're Doing This

In Liberia, access to technology and digital education resources remains limited. Many students and communities lack the basic tools needed to develop crucial computer skills in our increasingly digital world.

We believe that every dysfunctional laptop sitting in a drawer represents a missed opportunity. By refurbishing these devices with Linux and sending them to Liberia, we're not just recycling electronics, we're opening doors to education, connection, and future opportunities.

What Can You Donate?

We make donating easy. We accept laptops that are no longer useful to you but are full of potential for us.

✅ We Accept

  • Dysfunctional or slow Windows 10 laptops
  • Any Windows laptop (working or not)
  • Laptops of all brands and models
  • Devices that are old, slow, or have minor issues

🛡️ Our Promise

We perform a secure, certified data wipe on every device and install a free, open-source Linux operating system, transforming it into a perfect tool for learning.

The Impact of Your Donation

🎓 Empower a Student

Your donation provides a Liberian student with the tools they need to learn coding, research, and essential computer skills.

💚 Help the Planet

Reduce e-waste and give your technology a meaningful second life, supporting a circular economy.

💰 Get a Tax Refund

As an official non-profit, your laptop donation is tax-deductible. You will receive a receipt for your tax records.
